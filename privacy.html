Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what Level ("we", "us") collects when you use the Level website and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up or sign in, we store your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture with us.
  • Waitlist and newsletter. The email address you enter to join the waitlist or newsletter.
  • Contact form. The name and phone number you send us.
  • Card applications. The name, email, and business details you enter when you apply.
  • Site activity. Basic events such as page views, sign-ins, and button clicks, tied to the page they happened on.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To run the waitlist, process applications, and reply to your messages.
  • To understand how the site is used so we can improve it.

Where it is stored

Your information is stored in Supabase, our database provider. Waitlist and contact submissions also trigger an email notification to our team through Formspree. If you sign in with Google, Google processes that sign-in under its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time by emailing us.

Contact
